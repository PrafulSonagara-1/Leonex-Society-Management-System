v4.0.0 Community Suite & Navy Design System Latest

August 26, 2026
  • 🎨 New design system: deep navy/slate surfaces with a royal-blue accent, rebuilt around one token system with full Light, Dark and System theme support.
  • 🧩 SHUI component library: KPI stat cards, searchable/sortable/paginated tables, timelines, badges, empty states, skeleton loaders, confirm dialogs and toasts across every portal.
  • 💸 Expenses module: society expense ledger with categories and monthly totals for transparent committee accounting.
  • 📅 Events module: create, promote and track society gatherings and festivals.
  • 📁 Documents module: central repository for society records, bylaws and forms.
  • 🗳️ Polls upgrade: community voting with double-vote protection via per-poll voter lists.
  • 🚨 Emergency SOS: raise and resolve society-wide emergency alerts, surfaced across all portals.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Family members registry and 🅿️ parking slot management — assign and release slots with a visual slot grid.
  • 🔔 Notification center rebuilt: stored + derived notifications (gate visitors, unpaid bills, pending approvals), approve/deny actions right from the bell, mark-all-read and unread badges.
  • 🧾 Audit everywhere: every significant action now writes to the audit trail; monthly billing & collection stats power reports, plus a global search across all modules.
  • 🛠️ Complaint lifecycle refined: Open → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed, with full timeline entries on assignment and notes.
  • 🛡️ Visitor flow standardised: Guest, Delivery, Cab, Service Provider, Staff and Other types with Pending → Approved → Checked In → Checked Out | Rejected lifecycle.
  • 📱 Landing, docs & changelog refreshed on the new design system; PWA install and offline banner retained.

v2.5.0 Master SaaS UI/UX Overhaul

July 27, 2026
  • ✨ Floating Glassmorphic Navbar: Added floating header pill with smooth backdrop blur and scroll effects.
  • 🎨 Strict 60-30-10 Color Architecture: Implemented high-contrast input controls, slate card surfaces, and vibrant accent tokens.
  • 🔐 Static Google Auth Integration: Enhanced client-side Google sign-in fallback for static deployment.
  • 📱 Multi-Device Responsiveness: Full layout adaptation across smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens.
  • 👑 Role & Access Controls: Dynamic Grant/Revoke Admin controls in Admin settings panel.
